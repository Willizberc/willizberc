## 💼 Featured Projects

### 💰 Pexfin — Personal Financial Forecast Mobile App

**Role:** Lead Mobile Engineer & AI Integrator | **Stack:** React Native, Firebase, Gemini 1.5 API, TypeScript

Pexfin empowers users to gain clarity and control over their personal finances through AI-powered forecasting, budgeting, and smart insights. It predicts future income and expenses, helping users plan ahead.

**Highlights:**

* AI forecasting of income and expenses (3–6 months ahead)
* Gemini-powered chatbot for personal finance advice and budget analysis
* Budgeting, expense tracking, and goal-setting modules
* Interactive reports, charts, and smart notifications
* Firebase authentication and secure data storage

🚧 *In private alpha — refining ML accuracy and conversational flows before public launch.*

### 🚚 Ziplink Express — Logistics & Parcel Management Platform

**Role:** Lead Developer & System Architect | **Stack:** Django, TailwindCSS, PostgreSQL, AJAX, WebSockets

A full-scale logistics management system connecting vendors, riders, coordinators, and admins. Ziplink Express automates parcel creation, delivery tracking, and payment reconciliation.

**Highlights:**

* Dynamic parcel workflow and zone-based pricing engine
* Paystack and cash payment integrations
* Real-time tracking and rider assignment
* Admin/vendor dashboards and transaction summaries
* Notifications for parcel updates, payments, and assignments
* Deployed on Render (staging) and Azure App Service (production)

✅ *Live in production — managing day-to-day delivery operations.*

### 🧾 KNK Palvelut — Employee Time Tracking & Payroll System

**Role:** Lead Full-Stack Engineer | **Stack:** React + TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, NestJS, Prisma ORM, PostgreSQL

Built for a Finnish cleaning company, KNK Palvelut automates attendance, payroll, and work-site management across Admin, Supervisor, and Employee roles.

**Highlights:**

* Real-time clock in/out with automatic overtime calculation
* Automated payroll and salary computation
* Location-based assignments with grouped workplaces
* Authentication with token refresh & email verification
* Role-specific dashboards and weekly activity reports

✅ *Live in production*

### 💬 Bulk Messaging — SMS & Email Campaign Management System

**Role:** Full-Stack Developer | **Stack:** Django, TailwindCSS, PostgreSQL, Twilio API, SendGrid API

An internal communication system for managing large-scale SMS and email campaigns.

**Highlights:**

* Bulk message broadcasting with segmentation and personalization
* Campaign templates and scheduling tools
* Admin and CX user roles with permission control
* Real-time delivery analytics and engagement metrics
* Integrated Twilio (SMS) and SendGrid (Email) APIs
